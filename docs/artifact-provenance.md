# Dataset, model and notice provenance

Current source/rights evidence is tracked in
[#45](https://github.com/aaron-seq/Loan-eligibility-calculator/issues/45).
This is an evidence gap, not a finding of infringement or a legal approval.
The project's MIT code notice does not document the dataset publisher's terms.

| Artifact | Verified identity/history | Missing evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `data/train.csv` | 614 training rows; LF-normalized SHA-256 `3ac62ced374efaf4581a185a2456ed8288081e934d0841ad170f50a1d23ff835` | Original publisher/download/version, applicable terms and attribution, redistribution basis |
| `data/test.csv` | LF-normalized SHA-256 `2248e254edd4260a5cbae11856115d3d0336396239188e970c18c6e12dda4cd8` | Same source/terms evidence; it is not the trainer's held-out evaluation split |
| `models/loan_model.joblib` | Generated by the repository trainer from the training CSV; version/metrics in [model card](MODEL.md) and [metrics](../models/metrics.json) | Evidence supporting redistribution of the derived artifact |

Both CSVs are byte-identical after CRLF→LF normalization to the
[early repository snapshot](https://github.com/aaron-seq/Loan-eligibility-calculator/tree/afdd1528d1b5ecdd6afc93db55367078343326a2/data).
This identifies inherited files, not their original publisher or permissions.
The original README's links are provenance leads, not verified dataset terms.
No dataset was replaced or removed in this work.

That snapshot also contains an [MIT notice naming 2021 kunal zaveri](https://github.com/aaron-seq/Loan-eligibility-calculator/blob/afdd1528d1b5ecdd6afc93db55367078343326a2/ELICENSE).
The full historical notice is preserved in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md),
alongside Aaron's unchanged [current MIT notice](../LICENSE). This preserves
history without deciding which current material falls under either notice or
inferring a dataset license.

Before a new redistribution decision, identify the original source and
applicable terms, record required notices, and obtain the maintainer's
disposition for unresolved questions. Do not infer ownership, relicense files,
or claim unrestricted reuse from public availability. Larger replacement data
remains separate work in [#20](https://github.com/aaron-seq/Loan-eligibility-calculator/issues/20).
