## Verified distributor and download identity

Analytics Vidhya's [Loan Prediction practice problem](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/datahack/contest/practice-problem-loan-prediction-iii/)
exposes current training/test download URLs in its public `__NEXT_DATA__`
metadata. Its [ensembling tutorial](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2017/02/introduction-to-ensembling-along-with-implementation-in-r/)
references the older training download below. These primary sources identify
a distributor of matching data; they do not establish the original collector,
rightsholder or which URL the early repository author actually used.

| Official download | Comparison with committed file | Object `Last-Modified` (UTC) |
|---|---|---|
| [Older training CSV](https://datahack-prod.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/train_file/train_u6lujuX_CVtuZ9i.csv) | Exact match after CRLF→LF normalization | 2017-05-17 07:08:10 |
| [Current training CSV](https://datahack-prod.s3.amazonaws.com/train_file/train_ctrUa4K.csv) | Identical after also ignoring terminal LF bytes; all parsed fields equal | 2019-07-01 06:12:46 |
| [Current test CSV](https://datahack-prod.s3.amazonaws.com/test_file/test_lAUu6dG.csv) | Identical after also ignoring terminal LF bytes; all parsed fields equal | 2024-02-16 07:47:55 |

The orchestrator fetched each public download into memory, normalized only
CRLF to LF, compared bytes with and without terminal LF bytes, and separately
compared every row/field using Python's `csv.reader`. Headers plus 614/367 data
rows matched; no CSV rows were printed or added to the repository. Download
SHA-256 values after CRLF→LF normalization:

- Older train: `3ac62ced374efaf4581a185a2456ed8288081e934d0841ad170f50a1d23ff835`
- Current train: `5d396b7cfe5721e032af65c50f1963ed72e5a2639546026130ab64dd76f4e38c`
- Current test: `14b7d2ce5c5a7abe0fab03f250438e79dccd92a680a0bc6ed47a00ca5ad0a38d`

`Last-Modified` is observed object metadata, not a dataset release date or proof
of historical terms. No S3 version identifier was returned. The hashes identify
the downloads inspected on the check date; the URLs may change later.

## Remaining rights evidence and decision

The current [general terms](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/terms/) describe
restrictions on service/content use and refer to separate competition rules.
The reviewed public pages do not establish the historical terms applicable to
these CSVs or an explicit grant to redistribute them or the derived model.
Matching a public download and preserving a code notice do not resolve that gap.
No account was created, competition entered or permission obtained in this work.

Before a new redistribution decision, establish the applicable source/terms,
record required notices and obtain the maintainer's disposition. Do not infer
ownership, relicense files or claim unrestricted reuse from public availability.
Larger replacement data
remains separate work in [#20](https://github.com/aaron-seq/Loan-eligibility-calculator/issues/20).
