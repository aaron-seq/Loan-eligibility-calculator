A calibrated estimate

You get a probability, not a verdict — and we publish how well that probability tracks reality, so “72%” means something.

Reasons, not a black box

Every result breaks down which parts of your application pushed the estimate up or down, and by roughly how much.

What to change

We re-score realistic variations — borrow less, stretch the term, add a co-applicant — and show which ones actually move the number.

What this is honest about

  • The model is trained on a small public dataset of 614 applications. It is a demonstration, not an underwriting system.
  • It does not use your gender or marital status. Those are prohibited grounds for a credit decision, and we measured that leaving them out costs nothing.
  • Your credit bureau score is used in published, deterministic rules — not fed to the model, because the training data contains no bureau scores.
  • No lender sees this, and no lender is bound by it.
